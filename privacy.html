1. Agreement to YouTube Terms of Service

By using Galaxy.FM, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Galaxy.FM uses the YouTube API Services and your use of those services is subject to YouTube's Terms of Service.

2. YouTube API Services

Galaxy.FM uses the YouTube Data API v3 to search for music videos by title and artist, display video thumbnails and titles, and allow users to build a personal music library. We access YouTube API Services on your behalf when you search for songs or browse trending music.

3. Information We Access, Collect and Store

Galaxy.FM accesses and stores the following information:

  • YouTube API Data: Video titles, channel names, video IDs, and thumbnail URLs retrieved from the YouTube Data API. This data is stored locally in your browser (localStorage and IndexedDB) and may be cached for up to 30 days, after which it is refreshed or deleted.
  • Search queries: Song titles and artist names you search for, used solely to query the YouTube API and return results.
  • Music library data: Your personal playlists, liked songs, and library preferences, stored locally in your browser.
  • Audio files: Songs you download are uploaded to Cloudinary (a cloud storage service) and associated with your session.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

Galaxy.FM stores data directly on your device using browser localStorage and IndexedDB. This includes your music library, playlists, search cache, and app preferences. We do not use third-party tracking cookies. YouTube API responses may be cached locally to reduce API usage, and this cached data is deleted or refreshed within 30 days in accordance with YouTube API policies.

5. How We Use Your Information

Information accessed or collected is used solely to:

  • Search YouTube for music on your behalf
  • Display search results and song information
  • Store your personal music library locally on your device
  • Upload and stream audio files via Cloudinary

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with any third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

6. Information Shared with Third Parties

Galaxy.FM uses the following third-party services that may receive your data:

  • Google / YouTube Data API v3 — receives your search queries to return music results. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudinary — receives audio files for cloud storage and streaming. Subject to Cloudinary's Privacy Policy.
  • Last.fm API — receives song titles and artist names to fetch album artwork.
  • LRCLIB — receives song titles and artist names to fetch synchronized lyrics.

7. Google Privacy Policy

Because Galaxy.FM uses YouTube API Services, Google's Privacy Policy applies to the data processed through those services. You can view Google's Privacy Policy at: http://www.google.com/policies/privacy

8. YouTube API Data Refresh and Deletion

In accordance with YouTube API Services Developer Policies, Galaxy.FM does not store YouTube API data (such as video statistics, titles, or thumbnails) for more than 30 days. Cached search results are automatically cleared within 30 days. Users can manually clear all stored data at any time through the app's Settings panel.

9. Data Deletion

You can delete all data stored by Galaxy.FM at any time by:

  • Using the "Clear all data" option in the Galaxy.FM Settings panel
  • Clearing your browser's localStorage and IndexedDB through your browser settings
  • Uninstalling the Galaxy.FM app

To revoke Galaxy.FM's access to YouTube API data, visit Google Security Settings.
